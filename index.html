About Me

What is a Foreign Legal Consultant? A Foreign Legal Consultant is admitted in Arizona to advise on the law of a foreign country. Such a consultant is authorized to provide legal guidance only on foreign law (for example, the laws of India) and is not authorized to practice United States or Arizona domestic law.

About this practitioner — Sarabjeet Singh is admitted in Arizona as a Foreign Legal Consultant authorized exclusively to advise on Indian law only. He is NOT authorized to practice, advise on, or represent clients in United States or Arizona domestic law matters. He combines nearly 19 years of practice in India with admissions-based recognition in Arizona to assist clients with matters governed by Indian law and India–United States cross-border legal and business matters involving Indian law aspects.

Mr. Singh earned his law degree from Vikram University, Ujjain (2007) and was enrolled with the Bar Council of Punjab and Haryana in 2007. He has significant experience across civil and commercial litigation, property and land disputes, municipal and urban development law, regulatory compliance, and contractual matters.

Previously, Mr. Singh served as Empaneled Counsel for the Municipal Corporation Ludhiana, the Ludhiana Improvement Trust, and the Greater Ludhiana Area Development Authority, where he advised on land, development, and government-related legal issues.

Based in Arizona, Mr. Singh assists individuals, businesses, and legal professionals who require expertise in Indian law. He focuses on clear, practical legal advice, cross-border coordination, and helping clients navigate complex international matters with professionalism and integrity.
