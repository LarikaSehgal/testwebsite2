About Me

Sarabjeet Singh is a Foreign Legal Consultant based in Arizona with nearly 19 years of professional legal experience in India. He obtained his law degree from Vikram University, Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh, India, in 2007, and was enrolled with the Bar Council of Punjab and Haryana in 2007.

Mr. Singh has practiced law in Punjab, India, and has developed extensive expertise in a wide range of legal areas, including civil and commercial matters, property and land disputes, municipal and development matters, regulatory issues, contractual matters, and matters involving public authorities.

Throughout his career, Mr. Singh served as Empaneled Counsel for the Municipal Corporation Ludhiana, Ludhiana Improvement Trust, and Greater Ludhiana Area Development Authority (GLADA). These roles provided him with valuable experience in municipal administration, land and property issues, urban development, regulatory compliance, and the legal processes associated with government and public sector institutions.

As a Foreign Legal Consultant in Arizona, Mr. Singh assists individuals, businesses, and legal professionals with matters involving Indian law and India–United States cross-border legal and business issues. His practice focuses on providing reliable legal insights, facilitating international collaboration, and helping clients navigate the complexities of the Indian legal system.

Mr. Singh is committed to professionalism, integrity, diligence, and client service. He brings a strong ethical foundation, deep legal knowledge, and an international perspective to every engagement.
